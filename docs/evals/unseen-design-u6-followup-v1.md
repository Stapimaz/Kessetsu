Status: frozen before follow-up candidate generation on 2026-09-14. This protocol preserves the original U6 specification and its three unsupported Kessetsu attempts. It changes only the supplied Kessetsu capability: commit `1128e1b` adds a typed, hash-bound external-subcircuit reference. ## Common prompt

> Design a non-inverting gain-five amplifier around the authentic Texas Instruments `OPAx197` PSpice macromodel supplied locally as `./models/OPAx197.LIB`. Use exactly one OPAx197 instance and exactly three resistors: a two-resistor non-inverting feedback divider plus a fixed 10 kohm output load. The immutable supplies are +6 V and -6 V; the immutable input is a 100 mV-peak, 1 kHz sine that is also a 1 V AC stimulus. Gain at 1 kHz must be from 4.75 through 5.25; settled output peak and independently integrated fundamental amplitude must each be from 0.475 V through 0.525 V. The independent evaluator runs OP, AC from 10 Hz through 1 MHz at 100 points per decade, and a maximum-2-us-step transient through 10 ms, measuring the final five periods. Do not change or omit the source, supplies, load, topology, authentic model identity/content, pin order, analyses, or limits. Substituting a generic model is not success. The model is TI `OPAx197 PSpice Model (Rev. D)`, Final 1.3 dated 2022-06-23, downloaded from `https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/SBOMA34`; its required SHA-256 is `fc5b020e63346e511bd808bf41c856b0150b000bcf8a41fe00eeececb1f422a5`, and its pin order is `IN+ IN- VCC VEE OUT`. It is supplied only for this local evaluation under TI terms; retain its notices and do not copy, rewrite, embed in another artifact, or claim redistribution rights. Work only in the supplied directory and do not use the network. You may calculate and simulate iteratively. Preserve each materially different candidate revision in `revisions/` before replacing it. Write the final circuit to `candidate.kess`. Produce the schematic and editable/export artifacts supported by the supplied workflow. Finish with a short account of calculations, checks, model provenance, capability limits, and produced files. Do not inspect parent directories, evaluator code, repository examples, prior attempts, or another arm's work.

## Kessetsu tool reference

- Executable: `./kess.exe`; exact model sidecar: `./models/OPAx197.LIB`; final source: `candidate.kess`.
- Declare the external model exactly as: `external_subcircuit opamp OPA197 (in_p,in_n,vcc,vee,out) file="models/OPAx197.LIB" entry=OPAx197 sha256=fc5b020e63346e511bd808bf41c856b0150b000bcf8a41fe00eeececb1f422a5 version="Final 1.3" license="TI terms" source="https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/SBOMA34" simulator=ngspice_ps redistribution=prohibited`.
- Use `source`, `resistor`, and `opamp U1 OPA197`. Pins are source `plus/minus`, resistor `p1/p2`, and op-amp `in_p/in_n/vcc/vee/out`. Connect pins with `connect A.pin,B.pin to NET`. A +6 V rail uses a 6 V source from VCC to GND; a -6 V rail uses a 6 V source from GND to VEE.
- The input declaration is `source VIN sine_ac(0V,100mV,1kHz,1V)`. Required analyses are `simulate op`, `simulate ac dec 100 10Hz 1MHz`, and `simulate tran 2us 10ms`.
- Useful commands are `./kess.exe check candidate.kess --format json`, `compile`, `simulate --include datasets`, `render` to SVG/PNG, and `export --target schematic-json|spice|kicad|ltspice`. Keep every output beside `candidate.kess`; existing outputs require `--force`.
- Produce at least `candidate.svg`, `candidate.png`, `candidate.schematic.json`, `candidate.spice`, `candidate.kicad_sch`, and `candidate.asc`. These artifacts may retain typed provenance or a relative sidecar reference but must not contain the manufacturer model body. Do not copy or rename the model file.
- Kessetsu's PASS covers the declared model and simulated operating conditions only. It does not establish tolerance, board, thermal, EMC, production, or hardware performance.

## Evidence retained per attempt

The existing isolated runner is invoked with `-Protocol u6-external-v1 -Task U6 -Arm kessetsu`. It refuses overwrite, verifies the official model hash before inference, records Codex/Kessetsu/Ngspice/evaluator hashes and usage, retains the prompt, trace, revisions, candidate and exports in the gitignored evidence tree, and runs the versioned follow-up evaluator. The evaluator compiles the file-based source, requires the exact `kessetsu.models.v2` external-model metadata and one canonical relative include, then removes that candidate include and independently supplies the same hash-verified model to its own OP/AC/transient testbench. Manufacturer model text is excluded from committed evidence and reports.

Each of three attempts uses a fresh ephemeral `gpt-5.6-sol` session at medium reasoning, ChatGPT authentication, no API key, no network, at most 60 tool calls, and at most 30 minutes. A missing or altered model, generic substitution, wrong pins/runtime/redistribution policy, changed immutable circuit requirement, failed measurement, missing artifact set, or model-body leakage prevents the follow-up gate from passing.
